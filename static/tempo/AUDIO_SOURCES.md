# Audio sources for the inference-time split videos

- Engine (option A): "Car Engine: Maserati GranTurismo Switch to Sport Mode and Launch" by myeclecticself,
  Freesound #585215, https://freesound.org/people/myeclecticself/sounds/585215/ — CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  Used: the idle at 0.3–4.8 s, denoised (spectral subtraction, 30 Hz high-pass, 8 kHz low-pass), looped, and played
  back at a variable speed that follows the executed speed-up (tools/page_v19/v36_tempo_audio.py).
- Music box (option B): synthesized in tools/page_v19/v36_tempo_audio.py; no recorded material.
